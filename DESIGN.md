## Brand & Style

The visual narrative balances high-fashion editorial prestige with surgical operational utility. Tailored for commercial, portrait, and fine-art photography studios, the interface bridges the world of archival print catalogs and high-velocity workflow management.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Archival Elegance:** Emulates gallery exhibition monographs, bespoke linen prints, and medium-format contact sheets.
- **Architectural Poise:** Restrained, disciplined margins combined with deliberate typographic scale to inspire quiet confidence.
- **Instrumental Precision:** Dense schedules, shoot milestones, and financial contracts remain ultra-scannable, sharp, and utilitarian.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial minimalist foundation featuring warm linen surfaces, deep graphite structural lines, and warm amber gold focal anchors. Surfaces favor low-contrast natural boundaries over heavy drop shadows, allowing full-bleed proofing assets and status tokens to command attention without visual fatigue.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in tactile, organic tones reminiscent of darkrooms, linen photo mounts, and gold foil letterpress stamps.

### Core Canvas & Structure
- **Linen Base (`#FDFBF7`):** Primary window canvas and ambient background.
- **Warm Surface (`#F4EFEA`):** Card panels, table rows, and secondary structural containers.
- **Subtle Partition (`#EAE4DC`):** Recessed wells, form field fills, and alternating list layers.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5DFD7`):** Delicate boundary divisions between modules and canvas tiles.
- **Obsidian Graphite (`#1A1918`):** High-density text, primary headers, button fills, and structural chrome.
- **Muted Charcoal (`#5E5A54`):** Metadata, secondary captions, table column labels, and timestamp entries.

### Accents & Semantic Status Tokens
- **Amber Gold (`#C59B27`):** Primary brand accent, selected states, key dates, and urgent milestones.
- **Agendada (Scheduled):** Deep Prussian Blue (`#1E2C3D` text on `#E8EEF5` surface with `#C6D4E3` border).
- **Realizada (Completed / Shot):** Earth Sage (`#22382C` text on `#EAF1ED` surface with `#C5D8CD` border).
- **Entregada (Delivered):** Warm Amber (`#614502` text on `#FAF3E0` surface with `#E4D19E` border).
- **Cancelada (Canceled):** Neutral Ash (`#4D4842` text on `#ECE8E3` surface with `#D8D2CA` border).
- **Alerta Pendiente (Action Required / Past Due):** Crimson Oxide (`#5C1D1D` text on `#F9EBEB` surface with `#E8C4C4` border).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intentional tension between the romantic, high-contrast transitional serif **Playfair Display** and the austere, pragmatic sans-serif **Inter**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Reserved for studio client names, collection titles, invoice hero values, and primary view headers. Always rendered in weights 500 or 600 with slightly tightened tracking to emphasize craft.
- **Instrumental UI & Body (Inter):** Deployed across table matrices, calendar dates, metadata tags, and client communication threads. Tabular numbers (`tnum`) must be enforced on all financial ledgers, camera settings (ISO, Shutter, Aperture), and session countdowns.
- **Micro-Labels (Inter):** Rendered uppercase with generous letter tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to echo fine art gallery plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced 12-column grid provides horizontal discipline, paired with an 8px vertical baseline rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width bounded at `1600px`. Outer margins set to `2.5rem` with `1.5rem` gutters. Layout handles a persistent 280px left navigation spine alongside a multi-pane shoot inspector.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. Navigation collapses into an icon-rail (72px) or off-canvas drawer. Outer margins adjust to `1.5rem` with `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Modals convert into bottom sheets. Split-view panes (e.g., Shoot List + Shoot Details) transition into dedicated full-screen transitions with `1rem` outer margins.

### Whitespace Density Philosophy
Administrative workspaces (Client CRM Tables, Shot Lists, Rate Cards) follow compact padding intervals (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize visible information density. Presentation views (Client Gallery Previews, Contract Summaries, Invoice Proofs) expand into generous breathing room using `space-xl` and `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead creating dimension through architectural planes, tone-on-tone stepping, and crisp structural borders.

### Surface Tiers & Stacking
- **Ground Tier (Canvas):** `#FDFBF7` serves as the canvas substrate.
- **Tier 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** `#F4EFEA` layered over the base with a `1px` border of `#E5DFD7`.
- **Tier 2 (Popovers & Flyouts):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-subtle, warm ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Lightboxes):** `#FFFFFF` elevated above a rich charcoal translucent scrim (`rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.45)` with `2px` blur). Shadow definition: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.12)`.

### Border Discipline
Divisions between table columns, calendar time blocks, and card headers use a crisp `1px` solid border (`#E5DFD7`). Avoid multiple stacked borders; delineate layout hierarchy by alternating `#FDFBF7` and `#F4EFEA`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework favors architectural discipline. Corners are soft (`0.25rem` / `4px`) rather than rounded or bubbly, preserving the refined feel of traditional bespoke printing mounts and tailored linen folders.

### Radius Scale
- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Checkboxes, Micro Badges):** `4px` (`roundedness: 1`).
- **Container Elements (Cards, Panels, Modals, Flyouts):** `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted entirely to numeric count badges and circular portrait avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Graphite Luxe):** Solid `#1A1918` background, `#FDFBF7` text, `4px` radius, `0.5rem 1rem` padding. On hover: shifts to `#2E2C2A`. On active: subtle scale compression (`0.99`). Focus ring: `2px` offset with `#C59B27`.
- **Secondary (Linen Framing):** `#F4EFEA` background, `#1A1918` text, `1px solid #E5DFD7`. On hover: `#EAE4DC` background and `#1A1918` border.
- **Gold Accent (High-Value Actions):** `#C59B27` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Deployed sparingly for primary financial conversions (e.g., "Aprobar Presupuesto", "Cobrar Anticipo"). Hover: `#B38A1F`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, `#5E5A54` text. On hover: `#F4EFEA` fill, `#1A1918` text.

### Status Badges
Rendered at `11px` uppercase with `0.05em` tracking, `4px` radius, and `2px 8px` vertical/horizontal padding.
- **Agendada:** Text `#1E2C3D`, Background `#E8EEF5`, Border `#C6D4E3`.
- **Realizada:** Text `#22382C`, Background `#EAF1ED`, Border `#C5D8CD`.
- **Entregada:** Text `#614502`, Background `#FAF3E0`, Border `#E4D19E`.
- **Cancelada:** Text `#4D4842`, Background `#ECE8E3`, Border `#D8D2CA`.
- **Alerta Pendiente:** Text `#5C1D1D`, Background `#F9EBEB`, Border `#E8C4C4`. Dot indicator uses solid `#B83232`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF` (or `#FDFBF7` on modal surfaces), border `1px solid #E5DFD7`, text `#1A1918`, placeholder `#9E988F`. Active focus transitions border to `#1A1918` with a subtle `1px` inner box-shadow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px x 16px` frame, `1.5px solid #D8D2CA`, `3px` corner radius for checkboxes. Checked state fills with `#1A1918` displaying a white checkmark icon.

### Data Tables & Booking Rows
- **Table Headers:** `36px` height, uppercase `11px` Inter (`#5E5A54`), letter-spacing `0.06em`, border-bottom `1px solid #E5DFD7`, subtle linen background `#F4EFEA`.
- **Rows:** `52px` standard height, white or `#FDFBF7` alternating fill, hover state `#F5F0EB`. Bottom border `1px solid #EFEAE4`. Client names use Playfair Display (`15px`, weight 600). Date and financial values use Inter Tabular Numbers.

### Cards & Studio Milestone Panels
Constructed with `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFEA` fills and bordered with `#E5DFD7`. Card headers feature Playfair Display (`18px`–`20px`) with right-aligned status badges or action menus. Sections are cleanly partitioned using `1px` horizontal rules tinted with `#EAE4DC`.
